# Hydraulic System Troubleshooting Guide

## Overview

The hydraulic system provides the force required for the M-104 hydraulic
press.

The system depends on correct hydraulic fluid levels, pump operation,
pressure regulation, and proper cooling.

## Hydraulic Pressure Reference

The verified manufacturer pressure range, measurement units,
and sensor location have not been established for this
demonstration.

Confirm the pressure sensor configuration and consult
approved hydraulic documentation before interpreting a
reading as safe or unsafe.

Do not invent pressure limits or assume the units.

## Low Hydraulic Pressure

Possible causes include:

- Hydraulic fluid leakage
- Low fluid level
- Pump degradation
- Restricted hydraulic lines
- Valve malfunction

## High Hydraulic Temperature

High hydraulic temperature may occur when:

- Cooling performance is reduced
- Hydraulic fluid circulation is restricted
- Machine load increases
- Hydraulic components operate inefficiently

## Hydraulic Pressure and Temperature Relationship

When hydraulic pressure changes together with increasing temperature,
maintenance personnel should inspect both the hydraulic and cooling systems.

A pressure reduction combined with increasing temperature can indicate
degraded system performance.

## Troubleshooting Procedure

1. Check hydraulic fluid level.
2. Inspect hydraulic lines for leakage.
3. Check hydraulic pump operation.
4. Verify pressure readings.
5. Inspect cooling-system performance.
6. Review recent maintenance activity.
7. Compare current symptoms with historical incidents.

## Recommended Action

If pressure readings show a persistent or abrupt change relative
to comparable operating conditions, especially when temperature
is also increasing, notify qualified maintenance personnel.

Reduce machine load or initiate a controlled shutdown only in
accordance with the approved machine and site safety procedures.

Do not infer a safe or unsafe pressure limit from this guide.
Consult verified manufacturer specifications and approved
hydraulic documentation.
